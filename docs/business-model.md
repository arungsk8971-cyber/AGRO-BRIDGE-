# Business Model

## 1. Value Proposition

Agro Bridge aligns incentives for both sides of the agricultural supply chain:
- **For Farmers**: Assured daily market-linked rates, transparent digital weighing and A/B/C grading, zero middleman brokerage, reduced spoilage (<6% target in demo benchmarks), fast UPI/IMPS settlement, and affordable farm inputs deductible directly from pending payouts.
- **For B2B Buyers (Retailers, HoReCa, Processors)**: Consistent graded lots sourced directly from collection hubs with predictable arrival windows and consolidated interstate logistics.

---

## 2. Revenue Streams

1. **Inter-Market Logistics & Aggregation Margin**:
   Agro Bridge captures a portion of the surplus-to-deficit price spread (after paying farmers above the local mandi floor and covering collection, grading, crate handling, and highway freight).
2. **Value-Added Grading & Cold-Chain Handling**:
   Standardized sorting and ventilated crate packaging reduce transit bruising and unlock Grade A realization with institutional buyers.
3. **Farm Inputs Marketplace (`Shop`)**:
   Direct sale of verified fertilisers (`Urea`, `DAP`, `NPK 19:19:19`), crop protection (`Neem oil`, `Mancozeb`), `Seeds`, and `Drip irrigation line` kits:
   - Flat `₹80` last-mile delivery on orders below `₹2,000`, and **free delivery** at or above `₹2,000`.
   - **Payout-Adjusted Credit (`Adjust from payout`)**: Farmers with weighed, in-transit lots can procure inputs immediately against verified pending payouts without paying cash upfront.

---

## 3. Unit Economics Summary (Illustrative Demo Example)

For a `1,200 kg` Grade A Tomato lot (`AB-2291`):
- **Local Surplus Floor (Kolar)**: `₹8/kg` → `₹9,600`
- **Farmer Payout via Agro Bridge**: `₹9/kg` → `₹10,800` (`+₹1,200` extra farmer income)
- **Destination Market Wholesale Value (Chennai)**: `₹24/kg` → `₹28,800`
- **Gross Logistics & Margin Pool**: `₹18,000` (`₹15/kg` spread) covering hub handling, crate amortization, line-haul transport, shrinkage allowance, and platform margin.
