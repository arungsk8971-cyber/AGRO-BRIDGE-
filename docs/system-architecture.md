AGRO-BRIDGE — System Architecture

1. Overview

AGRO-BRIDGE is designed as an integrated digital and physical agricultural supply-chain system connecting farmers, buyers, collection centres, storage hubs, and logistics.

The platform will coordinate the movement of agricultural produce from procurement to final delivery while providing transparency, inventory visibility, and market intelligence.

2. Main System Components

A. Farmer Interface

- Farmer registration and login.
- Produce listing with crop, quantity, and availability.
- Daily market-linked pricing.
- A/B/C quality grading.
- Collection booking and order tracking.
- Payment and transaction records.

B. Buyer Interface

- Buyer registration and login.
- Browse available agricultural produce.
- Compare prices, quantities, and quality.
- Place bulk orders.
- Schedule delivery.
- Track orders and view transaction history.

C. Collection Centres

- Receive produce from farmers.
- Verify quantity and quality.
- Grade and record inventory.
- Organize temporary storage.
- Coordinate movement to buyers or district hubs.

D. District Storage Hubs

- Manage regional inventory.
- Provide commodity-specific storage.
- Consolidate produce for bulk distribution.
- Coordinate supply with buyer demand.

E. Logistics Management

- Allocate vehicles and transport partners.
- Plan delivery routes.
- Calculate transportation costs.
- Track shipments and delivery status.
- Coordinate nearby and inter-state distribution.

3. Core System Workflow

Farmer Registration → Produce Listing → Quality Verification → Procurement → Collection Centre → Inventory Management → Market-Balancing Analysis → Transportation Planning → Buyer Order Fulfilment → Delivery.

Direct delivery from collection centres will also be supported when it is more efficient.

4. Market Intelligence Layer

The market intelligence system will evaluate:

- Regional crop prices.
- Crop availability and surplus quantities.
- Buyer demand and destination prices.
- Storage capacity and availability.
- Transportation and handling costs.
- Expected distribution margins.

Based on these factors, the system will recommend commercially viable markets for distribution.

5. Data Management

The platform will maintain structured records for:

- Farmers and buyers.
- Produce listings and quality grades.
- Procurement and sales transactions.
- Inventory and storage.
- Orders and deliveries.
- Regional prices and market demand.
- Logistics expenses and operational costs.

6. Proposed Technology Stack

- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite.
- Backend: To be finalized based on product requirements.
- Database: To be finalized.
- Market Intelligence: Regional price comparison and distribution-cost analysis.
- Integrations: Future payment, mapping, logistics, and market-data services.

7. Scalability

The architecture will initially support operations in one taluk. It is designed to expand through additional collection centres, district storage hubs, buyers, and logistics networks.

As the platform grows, centralized data and market intelligence will help coordinate agricultural distribution across districts and states.

8. Long-Term Vision

Build a connected agricultural technology and infrastructure network that integrates software, storage, logistics, and market intelligence to make agricultural supply chains more efficient and transparent.

Development Status: Proposed system architecture. Components and integrations will be implemented and validated progressively through prototype development and pilot operations.
